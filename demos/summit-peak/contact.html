Basecamp questions

Does it actually snow?

A few mornings a year the granite wears a dusting, and it melts by lunch. The plows stay down in the valley, where they belong.

Can the dogs run the trail?

Leashed on the loop, loose in the run behind building two. The boot-wash by the gear room doubles as a paw-wash — we checked.

What about the drive after dark?

The highway is lit to the gate and the grade is gentle. Twenty minutes to downtown, star-lit the whole climb home.

Are garages available?

Every Granite plan includes a private two-car garage; Juniper and Ponderosa homes can add covered spaces by the building.
