The residences

Finished like a house you'd build yourself.

Every home at Ocotillo Ridge starts from the same conviction: the desert view deserves a room worthy of it. Interiors run quiet and warm — pale oak underfoot, deep counters, glass where the mountains are.

  • Custom walnut cabinetry

    Full-height uppers, soft-close everything, hardware in aged brass.

  • Honed quartz counters

    Waterfall islands in every one- to three-bedroom plan.

  • Smart home, standard

    Keyless entry, learning thermostats, and pre-wired shades — no upcharge.

  • Floor-to-ceiling glass

    South- and west-facing walls open to the ridgeline and the city grid.

Amenities

Made for the golden hour, and every other one.

  • Ridgeline pool

    A 75-foot lap edge aimed straight at the sunset, heated year-round.

  • Sunset terrace

    Fire tables, ramada shade, and the whole valley for a backdrop.

  • Fitness & yoga studio

    Strength floor, spin room, and a dawn-lit yoga deck.

  • Work-from-ridge suites

    Bookable offices and conference nooks with fiber to every desk.

  • Ramada kitchens

    Outdoor grills and long tables under mesquite shade.

  • Trailhead access

    A private gate onto the ridge loop — 4.2 miles of high desert.

  • EV charging

    Level-2 chargers in the garage, expandable as the community grows.

  • Pet run & wash

    A shaded run at the quiet end of the site, plus a proper wash bay.

The neighborhood

High ground, close to everything.

Ocotillo Ridge sits where the city meets the Catalinas — trailheads in one direction, Tucson's best dinner reservation in the other.

  • Sabino Canyon trailhead 8 min
  • La Encantada shops & dining 6 min
  • St. Philip's Plaza farmers market 10 min
  • University of Arizona 18 min
  • Downtown Tucson 20 min
  • Tucson International Airport 30 min
